# Detailed CUSAT model review

This is the full authoring model for Dhishna January 2027. The website now uses separate optimized desktop and mobile assets generated by scripts/optimize-model.mjs.

- Continuous leafy ground cover: 26,602 clumps, split into 42 animated patches.
- Shorter planting within the existing roundabout; statue moved to its centre at (0, -11).
- Separate statue island and its hedge removed; approach road remains clear.
- Reference-inspired CUSAT iron gateway replaces the temporary Dhishna banner; curved university lettering, pointed gate leaves and masonry piers.
- Thirteen additional trees frame the gateway and campus perimeter.
- Central statue figure enlarged 25% above its existing pedestal.
- Five layered palm crowns, irregular tree and shrub clusters, flowers and pebbles.
- Main building has six packed 2048 x 1024 image maps: four paint colour variants, a shared fine plaster normal map and a roughness map. World-scale facade mapping places fading, uneven plaster, rain trails, sill runoff and low damp marks across the building.
- Individual overlapping curved roof tiles, image-based material grain, carved gables, gutters and facade details.
- Slatted benches, parked bicycles and detailed preparation stalls.
- Eight-second wind loop uses rooted morph deformation rather than moving whole bushes.

Rebuild in Blender: open dhishna-campus.blend, then execute scripts/detail_scene.py, scripts/greenery_pass.py, scripts/palm_pass.py, scripts/gateway_pass.py, scripts/finish_gateway.py, scripts/weather_facade.py and scripts/export_detailed.py in that order. Run scripts/render_model_motion.py for the breeze preview.

Validation checks fixed plant roots and flag anchors, wind-loop closure, exported grass vertex colours, exported morph targets and weight animation channels.

The full review asset is approximately 56 MiB and 724,287 triangles. Keep it for editing. The public desktop and mobile exports have been optimized and browser-tested; see ../README.md and ../artifacts/optimization-report.json for web budgets. Physical phone performance still needs a device check.
